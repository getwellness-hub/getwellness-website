CONSENT

What healthy consent looks like

Healthy consent is voluntary, informed and specific. A person should be able to say yes or no without pressure, threats or manipulation.

Consent can change. Agreeing to one activity does not automatically mean agreeing to another, and consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Respecting boundaries is part of a healthy relationship. When there is uncertainty, pause and communicate rather than assuming.

If you have questions about a specific legal or medical situation, consult an appropriate qualified professional because laws and individual circumstances vary.
