What Healthy Consent Looks Like

Healthy consent is based on voluntary, informed and respectful communication. Understanding consent can help people respect boundaries and make relationships safer and healthier.

What Is Consent?

Consent means freely agreeing to something. In a healthy relationship, people should be able to communicate what they are comfortable with and make decisions without pressure, fear or manipulation.

Consent is not simply the absence of a refusal. It should be a clear and voluntary choice.

1. Consent Should Be Voluntary

A person should be able to make a decision freely. Consent is not healthy when someone is pressured, threatened, manipulated or made to feel that they cannot say no.

Remember: A respectful relationship allows both people to communicate their choices without fear of punishment or pressure.

2. Consent Should Be Informed

People need enough relevant information to make a meaningful decision. Important information should not intentionally be hidden when it could affect someone's choice.

Honest communication helps both people understand what they are agreeing to.

3. Consent Can Change

Consent is not necessarily permanent. A person can change their mind at any time.

Respecting a change of mind is an important part of respecting another person's autonomy and boundaries.

4. Saying No Is Okay

Everyone has the right to say no to something they do not want. A respectful response is to accept the decision without unnecessary pressure.

  • Listen when someone says no.
  • Do not repeatedly pressure them.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Give people space to make their own choices.

5. Communication Matters

Healthy consent involves communication. People can ask questions, express boundaries and make their preferences clear.

When something is unclear, asking respectfully can prevent misunderstandings.

6. Respect Personal Boundaries

Boundaries can be different for different people. They may relate to personal space, privacy, physical contact, communication or other parts of a relationship.

Healthy relationships do not require people to ignore their boundaries to please someone else.

7. Consent and Healthy Relationships

Respect for consent can strengthen trust. When people know that their choices and boundaries will be respected, communication can become more open and comfortable.

Consent is one part of a healthy relationship. Respect, honesty, communication and consideration for each other are also important.

Key Takeaways

Healthy consent should be voluntary, informed and respected. People should be able to communicate their choices, set boundaries and change their minds without unnecessary pressure.

Respecting another person's decisions is an important part of healthy communication and relationships.
