How to communicate better with your partner

Good communication is less about winning an argument and more about understanding each other.

Choose a calm moment. Speak about a specific situation instead of listing old problems. Use clear statements such as “I felt…” or “I need…” rather than accusations.

Listen without interrupting, repeat the important point you heard, and ask questions when something is unclear. If a conversation becomes heated, taking a short break can help both people return calmly.

Healthy communication takes practice. Small, consistent improvements are usually more useful than trying to solve every disagreement at once.
