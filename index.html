About Wellness & Relationships

Wellness & Relationships is an educational resource focused on practical, respectful and easy-to-understand information about relationships, communication, consent, privacy, boundaries and personal wellbeing. The goal is to help readers understand important topics and make informed choices for themselves.

Practical

Guides focus on useful ideas and everyday situations rather than complicated jargon.

Respectful

Content emphasizes mutual respect, personal choice, privacy and healthy communication.

Educational

Articles are designed to help readers learn, reflect and understand important wellbeing topics.
