# Content Rules — Wellness, Love & Relationships

## 1. Main Goal

Create and continuously improve useful, accurate, practical and
reader-focused educational content.

Content quality is more important than the number of articles.

---

## 2. Before Creating Any Article

The agent must first:

1. Check all existing article files.
2. Check existing article titles.
3. Check article topics and categories.
4. Identify overlapping or duplicate subjects.
5. Identify a genuine content gap.
6. Define the reader's likely search intent.
7. Decide whether a new article is actually necessary.

If an existing article already answers the topic well:

DO NOT create another duplicate article.

Improve the existing article instead when appropriate.

---

## 3. New Article Quality

Every new article should aim to have:

- Clear title
- Useful introduction
- Logical headings
- Practical explanations
- Easy readability
- Helpful examples where appropriate
- FAQ where useful
- Related internal links
- Appropriate SEO metadata
- Accurate information
- Trustworthy sources when factual claims require them

Do not create thin articles only to increase page count.

---

## 4. Article Structure

Use a clear structure such as:

Title
↓
Introduction
↓
Key points
↓
Practical guidance
↓
Important considerations
↓
FAQ when useful
↓
Related guides
↓
Conclusion

The structure can change when another format is more useful for the topic.

---

## 5. Accuracy

For health, sexual wellness, consent, safety, privacy,
relationship, legal or other factual topics:

- Prefer authoritative sources.
- Verify important claims.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Do not invent statistics.
- Do not invent studies.
- Do not invent quotations.
- Do not present speculation as fact.

When information can change over time, verify current information.

---

## 6. Sources

Use reliable sources where appropriate, such as:

- Government sources
- World Health Organization
- Recognized professional organizations
- Universities
- Peer-reviewed research
- Other authoritative institutions

Do not cite a source that was not actually consulted.

Do not fabricate references.

---

## 7. Reader Safety

Content must not encourage:

- Abuse
- Coercion
- Harassment
- Dangerous behavior
- Privacy violations
- Non-consensual behavior

For potentially sensitive topics, use respectful and educational language.

If a situation involves immediate danger or serious harm,
encourage appropriate professional or emergency support.

---

## 8. Relationship Content

Keep educational content balanced.

Do not assume every relationship is the same.

Discuss:

- Communication
- Respect
- Boundaries
- Consent
- Privacy
- Trust
- Emotional wellbeing
- Individual differences
- Safety

Avoid presenting personal opinions as universal facts.

---

## 9. Article Updates

Existing articles should be reviewed periodically.

Look for:

- outdated information
- broken links
- unclear explanations
- missing useful sections
- outdated sources
- weak introductions
- poor headings
- missing internal links
- unnecessary repetition

Only update when there is a meaningful improvement.

Preserve useful existing content.

---

## 10. Content Freshness

Do not change an article merely to make it appear new.

Update content when:

- reliable information has changed
- an important source has changed
- a section is genuinely outdated
- readers would benefit from clarification
- new relevant information materially improves the article

Record what changed and why.

---

## 11. Internal Linking

Add links when they genuinely help the reader.

Useful relationships may include:

Communication
↔ Trust
↔ Boundaries
↔ Conflict
↔ Emotional Connection

Consent
↔ Privacy
↔ Safety
↔ Digital Relationships

Do not add irrelevant links.

---

## 12. Categories

Use the website's established topic structure.

Current major categories include:

- Communication
- Relationships
- Marriage & Partnership
- Consent & Safety
- Privacy & Digital Life
- Sexual Wellness

Do not create unnecessary new categories.

---

## 13. Titles

Titles must:

- Clearly describe the article.
- Match the actual content.
- Be natural.
- Avoid clickbait.
- Avoid exaggerated promises.
- Avoid unnecessary keyword repetition.

---

## 14. Originality

Do not copy articles from other websites.

Create original explanations and structure.

When using external information:

- understand it
- verify it
- summarize it in original language
- cite the relevant source when appropriate

---

## 15. AI Content Quality

AI-generated content must be reviewed before publication.

The agent must check for:

- repetition
- generic filler
- unsupported claims
- awkward wording
- contradictions
- fake citations
- excessive headings
- unnatural keyword usage

Never publish content simply because AI generated it successfully.

---

## 16. Article Length

Article length should depend on the topic.

Do not make articles artificially long.

Prefer:

Complete answer
+
Useful details
+
Clear structure

over unnecessary word count.

---

## 17. Duplicate Prevention

Before publishing:

Compare the proposed article against existing articles.

Check:

- title similarity
- topic similarity
- search intent
- major sections
- key concepts

If substantial overlap exists:

STOP and evaluate whether updating the existing article is better.

---

## 18. Publication Safety

Before publishing a new article:

1. Verify filename.
2. Verify links.
3. Verify metadata.
4. Verify images and paths.
5. Verify HTML structure.
6. Verify internal links.
7. Verify sources.
8. Verify mobile readability.
9. Run quality checks.

If a critical check fails:

DO NOT PUBLISH.

---

## 19. Protected Website Assets

Never modify, rename, move or delete protected images.

Never remove existing working articles automatically.

Never change the website brand automatically.

Never change the domain automatically.

---

## 20. Continuous Improvement

The agent should continuously look for:

- better article ideas
- useful updates
- content gaps
- clearer explanations
- better internal linking
- improved FAQs
- improved readability
- better user experience

But:

If there is no meaningful improvement:

DO NOTHING.

---

## 21. Core Principle

Build a website that readers can trust.

Quality over quantity.
Accuracy over speed.
Useful content over filler.
Original explanations over copied content.
Reader value over search-engine manipulation.
Continuous improvement over unnecessary changes.
