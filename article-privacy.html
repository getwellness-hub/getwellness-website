Protecting intimate privacy online

Treat intimate photos and messages as highly private information. Share them only when you understand the risks and have clear consent from everyone involved.

Use unique passwords and two-factor authentication, keep your phone and apps updated, and review which apps can access photos, files or your camera.

Never forward another person's intimate material without their permission. If private material has been shared without consent, document what happened and consider reporting it to the relevant platform or authorities.

Digital privacy is an ongoing habit: think before sharing, limit access, and keep sensitive files out of public or shared accounts.
